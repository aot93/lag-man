How to mark

  1. Pick a frame where both strips are clear.
  2. Choose a region below, then click the four corners of that strip. The white cells at each end show where it starts and stops. Click in any order.
  3. Drag a corner to adjust it. The magnifier helps place it on the edge.
  4. When both regions read, save. Saving snaps the ends onto the cells.
